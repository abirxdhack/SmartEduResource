Privacy policy

This site collects as little as possible.

What is stored

  • Anonymous counters for how many times a document was opened or downloaded (no personal data).
  • Messages you send through the contact form (your name, email and message).
  • In your own browser only: theme choice and recently viewed documents.

Third parties

Documents are stored on Google Drive and are opened and downloaded directly from Google. Fonts load from Google Fonts and the optional Smart reader (PDF.js) from cdnjs; those providers may receive your IP address.
